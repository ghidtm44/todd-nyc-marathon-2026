Before you head out

  • No spectators at the start or on any course bridge. Choose a street-side spot and stay behind barriers.
  • The finish is controlled. Grandstands are ticketed; the standing area has screening and limited capacity.
  • Check travel on the day. Street closures and transit changes can affect your route. See NYRR’s spectator guidance and MTA service updates.
